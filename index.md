# SIARAN

SIARAN is a personal desktop/server tool I use to prepare and schedule my own YouTube
Shorts for the channel **The Handy Knotter** (@thehandyknotter).

What it does:

- takes a list of reference links I paste,
- downloads the source media for my own review,
- strips the original audio and replaces it with licensed background music,
- adds my own English narration, subtitles and branded step cards,
- uploads the result to my own YouTube channel on a schedule I approve.

It runs privately on my own hardware. It is not distributed, not sold, and no other
users have access to it.
